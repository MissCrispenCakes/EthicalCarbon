About

Built for neighbourhood-scale climate action.

EthicalCarbon is a resident-led initiative formed by collaborators already working across community air awareness, environmental sensing, public-interest data, climate and energy law, and creative public engagement.

We work in our own communities and across shared projects to make environmental information more participatory, accessible, and useful. Small teams can form around a local issue, place, or environmental question and work directly with residents, community partners, researchers, and subject-matter experts.

What we do
01

Observe

Neighbourhood walks, environmental sensing, resident observations, photographs, maps, and local knowledge.

02

Interpret

Workshops and community conversations connecting local conditions to climate, air quality, buildings, transportation, land use, resilience, and policy.

03

Share

Accessible maps, public displays, creative outputs, data stories, and practical climate education designed for non-specialists.

04

Remember

Community-created records that preserve what was observed, asked, learned, and changed over time — an environmental working memory.

Current direction

Neighbourhood Environmental Working Memory

A participatory climate-learning project combining neighbourhood observation, low-cost sensing, collaborative mapping, workshops, expert conversations, and creative public communication.

Residents help identify questions, collect observations, interpret what they see, and build a shared record that can be revisited throughout the year.

For communities, not laboratories.

Sensing tools are used for education and participation — not as regulatory monitors — and community knowledge remains part of the evidence.

Why EthicalCarbon

Local knowledge deserves infrastructure.

Climate action is easier to participate in when people can connect large systems to the places they already know: their streets, buildings, parks, transit routes, workplaces, and neighbourhoods.

EthicalCarbon is a place to do that work together.
